Identity

The mark, the type, the colours and the page, for everything that does not move: paper, slides, signs.

{{MARK}}

Mark

  • The mark is the production artwork (a 45 mm master, one path in one colour); it is scaled, never redrawn, simplified or corrected.
  • Its eyes are always white with ink rings and pupils: on ink the creature turns paper and the eyes keep their outline (the white master); on paper they sit in the creature's ink.
  • Clear space on every side: one eye (a fifth of the mark's width). Nothing else enters it, not even a rule. Smallest size: 5 mm (16 px) wide.

Page

Constraints

  • The mark is never stretched, rotated, outlined, shadowed or put in a box; it is never recoloured beyond ink and paper.
  • Images are figures: points and lines from the figure system, rendered still at print resolution. No stock photography, no gradients.
  • One weight of rule (the 2 px head rule, 0.5 pt in print) and hairlines; nothing else frames content.
  • Everything sits on the quarter line (1.25 mm in print). A layout that does not, is wrong, however good it looks.
