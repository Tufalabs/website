We're back from summer break, and **Tufa Talks** is returning! On September 30, we'll welcome **Max Rumpf**, co-founder and CEO of [SID](https://www.sid.ai/), for the fourth episode at Tufa Labs in Zürich.

Max leads an AI research lab focused on search and retrieval. His work explores search as a reinforcement learning problem, with the aim of giving AI systems the context they need to be useful. Before co-founding SID, he researched hardware-software co-design for machine learning systems in the SAFARI group at ETH Zürich.

Doors open at 17:45 and close promptly at 18:00. The talk will be followed by an open Q&A, then pizza, drinks, and time to catch up with the community.

Spots are limited and registration is subject to approval. We'd love to see you there!
