  • Structure (every rule and line of the page) is there from the first frame; only content appears. Every duration is τ·2k; every start is a whole or half beat. Consecutive pieces start at least ½τ apart; a page has arrived by 10τ.
  • A figure answers within a frame; a transition takes 4τ; a play lasts at most 40τ, then rests. Hover changes take τ.

Page

Constraints

  • Everything visible is a component or a composition of components. Ink only; no boxes or shadows.
  • Inside a figure: one main panel as large as the head allows (squares in whole lines, 4:3 in threes), anything secondary a strip of 1–3 lines under it at the same width, a line apart; equal panels sit side by side at one height. The group is centred in columns 8–12 and in the head; margins, lattices and offsets are ℓ/2 or ℓ/4.
  • Text ≥ 4.5:1 on the ground. Nothing travels more than 6px; no motion under reduced motion.
  • Below 900px: one column, no figures, nothing pinned. Press G for the grid.
